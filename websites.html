A clear information structure

AL-AQSA designed the content hierarchy and navigation around the group, its companies, and their work. Dedicated service, product, and project presentation helps visitors explore relevant capabilities and move toward a contact or proposal enquiry.

English and Arabic, across devices

The website provides a bilingual English and Arabic experience, responsive navigation, and mobile-friendly layouts. The interface supports both left-to-right and right-to-left reading so visitors can explore the group in their preferred language.

Project presentation and enquiry journeys

Project and case-study presentation gives the group’s work a dedicated place in the website. Clear calls to action connect company and service information with contact and proposal functionality, while careers and company information support other visitor needs.
