The challenge

Present the work of an engineering, contracting, and trading group operating in Qatar and Syria, while making information across its companies easy to find.

What AL-AQSA delivered

A responsive corporate website with company profiles, services, products, projects, careers, and contact/proposal functionality. English and Arabic navigation supports different audiences.

About Sahiony Group

Sahiony Group brings together engineering, contracting, and trading businesses operating in Qatar and Syria. The website presents the group and its companies within one coordinated digital presence, while making each company’s capabilities and work accessible to visitors.

Making a complex group easy to understand

The project needed to organize several companies and a wide range of business information without making visitors navigate disconnected websites. Company profiles, services, products, project examples, careers, and enquiries needed a clear place within the overall structure.

A clear information structure

AL-AQSA designed the content hierarchy and navigation around the group, its companies, and their work. Dedicated service, product, and project presentation helps visitors explore relevant capabilities and move toward a contact or proposal enquiry.

English and Arabic, across devices

The website provides a bilingual English and Arabic experience, responsive navigation, and mobile-friendly layouts. The interface supports both left-to-right and right-to-left reading so visitors can explore the group in their preferred language.

Project presentation and enquiry journeys

Project and case-study presentation gives the group’s work a dedicated place in the website. Clear calls to action connect company and service information with contact and proposal functionality, while careers and company information support other visitor needs.

AL-AQSA’s contribution

Website design and development, responsive layouts, UI/UX, information architecture, bilingual navigation, company and project presentation, and contact/proposal functionality. The focus was to turn complex business information into an accessible customer-facing experience.
